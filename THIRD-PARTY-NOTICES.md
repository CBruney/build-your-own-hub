# Third-party notices

The project has no project license file, as requested by its creator for this friends-and-colleagues starter. That choice does not remove the licenses attached to included third-party assets.

## Fonts

- Cormorant Garamond, distributed through `@fontsource/cormorant-garamond`, is included for editorial headings. Its SIL Open Font License and copyright notices are preserved in [cormorant-LICENSE.txt](src/assets/cormorant-LICENSE.txt).
- Manrope, distributed through `@fontsource/manrope`, is included for interface text. Its SIL Open Font License and copyright notices are preserved in [manrope-LICENSE.txt](src/assets/manrope-LICENSE.txt).

The bundled files are unmodified Latin WOFF2 subsets from the locally installed Fontsource packages. Keep the notices with redistributed font files and consult the attached terms before modifying font names or files.

The starter uses Unicode navigation symbols, no bundled icon library, no external images, and no runtime JavaScript dependencies. Its screenshots depict only fictional data.
